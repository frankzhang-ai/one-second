ONE SECOND – Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 9, 2026

This policy applies to the ONE SECOND website and its iOS and Android apps.

No account or personal information

ONE SECOND does not require an account or sign-up and does not collect personal information. Your best score and sound setting are stored only on your device using local storage.

Anonymous usage statistics

We use GoatCounter, a cookie-less analytics service, to count page views and anonymous game events such as game starts, game-over score ranges, and returning visits on a later day. We do not use cross-site tracking or advertising IDs, and analytics data is not sold or shared for advertising. GoatCounter may process your IP address and browser user agent transiently to count visits. Read GoatCounter’s privacy information.

Advertising and device features

ONE SECOND currently contains no ads. The apps use haptics only as optional tactile feedback; sound can be turned off in the game.

Children

ONE SECOND does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users.

Changes

This policy may be updated as the game changes. The effective date above will be revised when changes are made.
